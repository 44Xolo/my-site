Seven programmes. Start where you are.

Every lab runs the same way: small groups, real projects, and mentors who came through the programme themselves. See full programme details

  1. CODE

    Build websites and simple apps by making real tools for local clinics, shops and community groups.

  2. AI

    Understand how AI tools work, where they get things wrong, and how to use them responsibly.

  3. GREEN

    Hands-on solar and clean-energy training with real panels and installation practice.

  4. BUILD

    Design and make prototypes in the workshop, from first sketch to something you can hold.

  5. ENTREPRENEUR

    Turn an idea into a small business: pricing, finding customers and making a first sale.

  6. EDUCATION

    Learn to teach what you know and run sessions for younger learners in your area.

  7. CONNECT

    Weekend sessions where graduates teach others and keep the community growing.

From first session to teaching others

  1. Join a lab

    Sign up for a lab in Johannesburg or Durban. You do not need any experience.

  2. Learn by building

    Work in a small group on a real project from the first week.

  3. Ship something real

    Finish with a tool, a prototype or a business plan that someone actually uses.

  4. Teach it forward

    Come back as a mentor and lead a session for the next cohort.
