OUR STORY

We started in a container.

In 2019 in Dobsonville, one shipping container and 12 refurbished laptops became the first BASIC lab. Today there are 2 labs and 7 programmes.

Mission

Close the absorption gap with community-owned labs teaching skills that pay rent tomorrow.

Vision

A township where every 2 km has a Future Lab with power, WiFi and a volunteer who knows your name.

Core values

Access

Refurbished devices and free clinics.

Innovation

Practical AI, solar and code for local problems.

Community

Facilitators from the same streets as learners.
